# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want visually-3d to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. Contributors and maintainers pledge to
make participation a respectful and constructive experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, respectful, and patient.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. Please read it for the full set of guidelines and enforcement
guidance.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers at **kingmasatojames@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
